SME Cybersecurity Remediation Playbook

Plain-English, copy-paste remediation recipes for small and medium businesses without a dedicated security team.

1

Stop Domain Email Spoofing & Phishing (DMARC / SPF)

The Problem: Cybercriminals send fake emails posing as your CEO or billing department to deceive customers and employees into paying fraudulent invoices (Business Email Compromise).

Step 1: Publish DMARC TXT Record
v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@yourdomain.com;
Step 2: Enforce SPF Record
v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
2

Block Exposed Remote Desktop (RDP) & SMB Ransomware Vectors

The Problem: Over 70% of SME ransomware attacks begin with exposed RDP (port 3389) or SMB (port 445). Botnets scan the internet 24/7 trying default passwords.

Fix: Block at Perimeter & Enforce VPN
  • Delete port-forwarding rules for ports 3389 and 445 on routers.
  • Require employees to connect via a secure VPN (WireGuard or OpenVPN) before accessing internal desktops.
  • Enable Network Level Authentication (NLA) in Windows Remote Desktop settings.
3

Enforce HTTPS & Browser Security Headers (HSTS, CSP)

The Problem: Without HSTS, attackers can downgrade users from HTTPS to unencrypted HTTP to snoop passwords. Without X-Frame-Options, your web portal can be framed in Clickjacking scams.

Nginx Configuration Snippet:
add_header Strict-Transport-Security "max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains" always;
add_header X-Frame-Options "SAMEORIGIN" always;
add_header X-Content-Type-Options "nosniff" always;
add_header Referrer-Policy "strict-origin-when-cross-origin" always;
4

Deploy the 3-2-1 Immutable Backup Rule

The Problem: Modern ransomware actively hunts down and wipes connected backup drives before encrypting primary servers.

The 3-2-1 Strategy:
  • 3 Copies: Primary + 2 backup copies.
  • 2 Formats: Different storage media (NAS + Cloud).
  • 1 Offsite / Air-Gapped: Cloud copy with Object Lock (immutable against ransomware deletion).
